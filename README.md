**Automotive Incentive Performance Dashboard**

**What it tracks:** Monthly incentive payouts, dealer compliance, and sales performance across 5 Canadian regions and 8 automotive OEM manufacturers, using synthetic data modeled on real-world automotive incentive management workflows.

**Tools & techniques:** Built in Power BI with a star-schema data model (dedicated Date table with proper relationships), intermediate-to-advanced DAX including time-intelligence measures (month-over-month payout change via DATEADD), conditional aggregation for compliance rate calculations, and rule-based conditional formatting to flag at-risk and non-compliant dealers at a glance.

**Business outcome:** Surfaces underperforming dealers and payout variances before incentive periods close, enabling proactive intervention rather than after-the-fact reporting — mirroring the kind of incentive auditing and compliance tracking used in automotive OEM incentive management.
